Delete your App Testers Community account

App Testers Community (package com.apptesters)

Option 1: inside the app (instant)

  1. Open App Testers Community and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings > Privacy & Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email bilalhassanali@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, bio, photo, settings)
  • Apps you listed and their tester lists
  • Your testing history and check-ins
  • Notifications, push tokens, feedback and reports you sent
  • Files you uploaded (such as screenshots)
  • Your sign-in record

What may remain

Reports that other users filed about you may be kept for abuse prevention; they contain only an internal ID, not your name or email. Deletion cannot be undone.
