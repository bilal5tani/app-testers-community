Privacy Policy

App Testers Community (package com.apptesters). Last updated: 29 October 2026.

App Testers Community helps Android developers find testers for Google Play closed testing, and helps testers keep track of their daily check-ins. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

DataWhy
Google account name, email address and profile photo (when you sign in with Google)To create and secure your account.
Username, bio and profile settings you chooseTo show your profile to other users, as you allow in Privacy settings.
Google Play email, device model and Android version (optional)So app developers can add you to their tester list and know your test device. The Play email is shown only to developers of apps you join.
Apps you list (name, package name, links, description, icon)To let others find and test your app.
Which apps you test and your daily check-insTo show progress toward Google's 14-day closed testing requirement.
Push notification tokenTo send reminders and progress notifications to your device.
Reports, blocks and feedback you sendTo keep the community safe and let testers talk to developers.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

Who can see it

  • Other users see your display name, username, bio and avatar, unless you turn off "Public profile".
  • A developer sees the testers of their own app, including each tester's check-in history for that app and, to manage the Google Group, the tester's Play email.
  • Only you see your notifications and settings.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage (servers in South Korea).
  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) handle sign-in and push notifications.

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by row-level access rules so users can only read what they are allowed to see.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, apps, check-ins, notifications, feedback, reports you made, push tokens and uploaded files are permanently deleted.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details in Settings > Edit Profile.
  • Download your data in Settings > Privacy & Account > Export My Data.
  • Delete your account in Settings > Privacy & Account > Delete Account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
  • Turn individual notifications off in Settings > Notification Settings or in Android system settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
